Bring Clean Water to a Community

In Nigeria, 1 in 5 people don’t have clean water. Families spend hours every day walking to collect it — hours stolen from school, work, and life. One well changes all of that for an entire community.

Here’s what your gift provides:

  • $25provides 1 person with safe water purification supplies for a month
  • $55a family stops guessing if their water is safe and starts knowing
  • $100funds a household water filter for up to 10 neighborhood members

  • $500a step toward the borehole (a deep-drilled well) — safe water systems for an entire community, 100+ people
  • $1,000major contribution toward a community borehole serving an entire village, 300+ people

Why Water Matters

What if you had to go a day without access to clean, safe drinking water?

Imagine waking up every morning knowing that the water you drink could make you or your loved ones sick. For over 2 billion people worldwide, this isn’t just a worry; it’s their everyday reality.

At United in One Global Foundation, we’re committed to turning this around by bringing clean, safe drinking water to rural communities. Together, we can create a future where no one has to make the impossible choice between thirst and health.

How We Work

From what we can do now to what we are building next.

01

Listen First

We work with local leaders to understand each community’s water needs before offering any support.

02

Provide & Educate

We supply water purification tablets and teach families how to use them, making unsafe water safe to drink today.

03

Build Toward More

Our goal is to fund and construct wells and boreholes in the communities that need them most, moving from short-term relief to long-term access.

04

Support That Lasts

After a well is built, we’ll stay involved, providing ongoing support so clean water lasts.
